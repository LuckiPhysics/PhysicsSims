Notes · Box A

Three kinds of interactions

You'll set up the red and blue carts three different ways:

Box A: A collision starts apart. The carts either bounce apart or stick together. An explosion starts together and ends apart.

For every trial you'll find each cart's momentum before and after, then add them to get the total. Tomorrow's big question: what happens to the total?

Notes · Box B

Two carts, one total

Right is positive. Each cart's momentum keeps its sign, and the total is the sum with the signs:

ptotal = pred + pblue

Try it. Make the carts move toward each other. Can you make the total zero?

predpblueptotal
Box B: Add momenta with their signs. Two carts moving toward each other partly cancel, so the total can be smaller than either cart's momentum, or even zero.
Notes · Box C

Worked example: one row of the lab table

Trial 4, head-on: the red cart (1.0 kg) moves right at 0.50 m/s. The blue cart (0.50 kg) moves left at 0.50 m/s. Fill in the "Initial" half of the row.

Step 1: Signs first. Right is +, so vred = +0.50 m/s and vblue = −0.50 m/s.
Step 2: Red. pred = (1.0 kg)(+0.50 m/s) = +0.50 kg·m/s
Step 3: Blue. pblue = (0.50 kg)(−0.50 m/s) = −0.25 kg·m/s
Step 4: Total. ptotal = (+0.50) + (−0.25) = +0.25 kg·m/s. Same speeds, but the heavier red cart "wins," so the total points right.

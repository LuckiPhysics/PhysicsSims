Explosions and 3+ Objects

About 12 minutes · Due before class Tuesday 12/1

In ML5 two skaters pushed apart from rest. Tonight: explosions where the system is already moving, where the energy comes from, and what to do when there are three or more pieces.

Notes · Box A

Start together, end apart

An explosion is any interaction where objects start together and end up apart: a cannon firing, a spring launcher, a skater throwing a ball, a rocket dumping fuel.

Momentum: the push between the pieces is internal (Newton's 3rd law pairs), so the total momentum stays the same: Σpi = Σpf.
From rest: the total is zero before, so it's zero after. The pieces get equal and opposite momenta, and the heavier piece moves slower (a cannon's recoil).
Energy: total KE increases. It comes from stored energy: a compressed spring, chemical energy in gunpowder or fuel, or your muscles.
Box A: Explosion = start together, end apart. Total momentum is conserved (Σpi = Σpf). From rest: equal and opposite momenta. Total KE increases; it comes from stored energy (spring, chemical, muscles).
Notes · Box B

Explosions on the move

A 2.0 kg cart (A, red) carries a 1.0 kg block (B, blue). A spring on the cart launches the block. Set how fast they move together at first, and how fast the block leaves.

Right is +. Try a starting velocity of 0, then 2.0 m/s.

Quick check: Can both pieces move forward after an explosion?

Box B: If the system starts moving, the total momentum is not zero, and it stays the same after. The pieces do not have to go opposite ways. Start every problem with Σpi = (total mass)(starting velocity).
Notes · Box C

Three or more pieces: just add more terms

A 0.60 kg firework is at rest when it bursts into three pieces. A 0.20 kg piece flies at +30 m/s and a 0.10 kg piece flies at −20 m/s. Find the velocity of the 0.30 kg piece.

Write it out: Σpi = Σpf becomes 0 = p1f + p2f + p3f. One term per piece, however many pieces.
Plug in: 0 = (0.20)(+30) + (0.10)(−20) + (0.30)v3f = +6 − 2 + 0.30v3f
Solve: 0.30v3f = −4, so v3f ≈ −13 m/s (to the left). The third bar has to cancel the other two.
Box C: More objects = more terms: Σpi = p1f + p2f + p3f + … Keep the signs. The missing bar is whatever makes the total match.
Notes · Box D

Worked example: launching from a moving cart

A 25 kg cart carrying a 5.0 kg box rolls at +2.0 m/s. A spring launches the box forward at +8.0 m/s. Find the cart's velocity after, and the change in KE.

Before: Σpi = (25 + 5.0)(+2.0) = +60 kg·m/s. Not zero: they were already moving.
After: +60 = (5.0)(+8.0) + (25)vCf = +40 + 25vCf, so vCf = +0.80 m/s. Both move forward.
KE before: ½(30)(2.0)² = 60 J. KE after: ½(5.0)(8.0)² + ½(25)(0.80)² = 160 + 8 = 168 J.
Where did 108 J come from? The compressed spring. Explosions always gain KE from stored energy.

Sample bounce trial

Red (0.50 kg) moves at +0.60 m/s into blue (0.50 kg) at rest. After the bounce: red +0.03 m/s, blue +0.54 m/s.

ptotal before = (0.50)(0.60) + 0 = 0.30 kg·m/s. What is ptotal after?

kg·m/s

How close is "close"?

Before: 0.30 kg·m/s. After: 0.285 kg·m/s. Real data never match perfectly, so we use a percent difference:

% difference = |before − after| ÷ before × 100

What is the percent difference?

%

Why isn't it exactly the same?

The total went from 0.30 to 0.285 kg·m/s. What's the best explanation?

Sample stick trial

Red (0.50 kg) moves at +0.80 m/s into blue (1.00 kg) at rest. The Velcro makes them stick and move together.

If momentum is conserved, how fast should the stuck carts move?

m/s

What about kinetic energy?

Same stick trial. KE before = ½(0.50)(0.80)² = 0.16 J. KE after = ½(1.50)(0.267)² ≈ 0.053 J.

Your lab sheet asks: was KE conserved?

Sample explosion trial

Red (0.50 kg) and blue (1.50 kg) start at rest, pressed together. The plunger fires and red moves off at −0.90 m/s.

What velocity should blue have? Include the sign.

m/s

Systems and Energy Bar Charts

About 10 minutes · Review for the Energy reassessment

Every energy problem starts with two choices: what's in the system, and what forms the energy takes. A bar chart shows both at once, and it turns straight into the equation you solve.

Have your notes sheet out. When you see an orange Notes · Box A tag, fill in that box on your sheet. The diagrams are already drawn, so you only write the key ideas.

Notes · Box A

Choosing the system

A ball is dropped from rest. Ignore air resistance. Watch what happens to the bar chart when you change what's inside the system (the dashed boundary).

Box A: Same fall, same final K. Only the bookkeeping changes.
• Earth in the system: gravity is internal, and the energy starts as Ug.
• Earth outside: gravity is an external force that does positive work.
Ug only exists when the Earth is in the system.

Notes · Box A

Open or closed?

Once you've drawn the boundary, ask: does anything outside the system do work on something inside?

Closed systemOpen system
External workNoneYes (a push, friction from an outside surface, gravity if Earth is outside…)

Box A: Closed: Ei = Ef. Open: Ei + Wext = Ef. Energy is never created or destroyed. It only moves across the boundary as work.

Notes · Box C

Energy bar charts

  1. Name the system first.
  2. Initial bars: what forms are there at the start?
  3. Wext bar: zero if the system is closed. Below the line if energy leaves.
  4. Final bars: what forms are there at the end?
  5. The bars must balance: initial + Wext = final. Heights only need to be right relative to each other.

Example: A ball is rolled up a hill and stops partway up. Ignore friction.

Step 1: System. Ball + Earth. Nothing outside pushes on the ball and there's no friction, so the system is closed: Wext = 0.
Step 2: Initial. Put zero height at the bottom, so Ug = 0 there. The ball is moving, so the energy is all K.
Step 3: Final. At the highest point the ball is stopped (K = 0), so it's all Ug. Four blocks in, four blocks out.
Step 4: The chart is the equation. Each bar that isn't zero becomes a term:
½mvi² = mghf
Step 5: What if the hill has friction? Put the hill's surface in the system. Some K becomes Eth, so less is left for Ug. The ball doesn't get as high.
Box C: Initial + Wext = Final. Every nonzero bar becomes a term in the equation.

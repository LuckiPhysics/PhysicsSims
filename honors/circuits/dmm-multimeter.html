Virtual Multimeter Lab

About 15 minutes · Do this after the multimeter video

You'll use a virtual digital multimeter (DMM) on a battery-and-bulb circuit. You'll measure voltage and current the right way, and make the classic mistake on purpose, safely, so you never make it with a real meter.

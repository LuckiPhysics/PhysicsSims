Five rules before you touch anything

  1. Build with the power OFF. Only turn it on after your partner check.
  2. Never touch the top CURRENT knobs. Mr. Lucki set them. Use only the bottom VOLTAGE knobs.
  3. Use only the red + post and the black − post. Never the green GND post in the middle.
  4. Set the meter's dial before you hook it up. Never turn the dial while the meter is part of a powered circuit.
  5. Hold wires by the clips and turn the power off between readings. Resistors can get warm.

Graph hints: a new shape

You know TAILS. What's new is the Part 1 graph: it's your first inverse graph.

My points don't make a straight line

That's expected. Don't force a straight line, and don't connect the dots. Draw one smooth curve through the middle of your points.

How is this different from the graphs I've made?
  • GPE vs. height: a straight line. Double the height, double the energy.
  • KE vs. speed: curves up and gets steeper.
  • Current vs. resistance: starts high, drops fast, then flattens out. Bigger resistance → smaller current, but the drop gets smaller each time.
How do I describe the pattern with numbers?

Find two resistors where one is double the other (like 10 Ω and 20 Ω). What happened to the current? Try another pair. Then try triple. Question 1 asks for exactly this kind of ratio.

Should my curve hit zero?

Think about it: could any resistor make the current exactly zero, or just smaller and smaller? Let your curve get close to the axis without crossing it.

Question 2 (the 78 Ω resistor)

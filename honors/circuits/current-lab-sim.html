Need a hint? How the circuits go together
  • Drag the ends of the leads from the tray. Banana plugs go into posts and jacks. Alligator clips grab resistor wires (or a post). Ends snap on when they get close.
  • Tap the green POWER switch, the arrows under the VOLTAGE knobs, a meter's dial, or the resistor (⇄) to change them.
  • Part 1 loop: + post → meter (dial on 10A, leads in 10ADC and COM) → resistor → − post.
  • Part 2 loop: + post → unknown resistor → ammeter → 10 Ω resistor → − post. The voltmeter (dial DCV 20, leads in VΩmA and COM) goes across the 10 Ω resistor.
  • One meter in Part 2? Record all the currents first. Then replace the meter with a yellow jumper, switch it to a voltmeter, and record VR at the same supply voltages.
  • Every step and mistake is explained on the Current Lab help page.
